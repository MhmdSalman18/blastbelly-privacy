Who we are

Blastbelly (Android package com.salnovaabs.blastbelly) is a game provided by SalNova Labs. This Privacy Policy explains information stored or processed when you play the game, contact us, or open this policy page.

Privacy contact: salnovalabs183547@gmail.com. Effective date and last updated: 18 October 2026.

Your progress on your device

The game stores checkpoints, chapter completion and unlocks, collected beans, activated level devices, best height, the highest chapter reached in a climb, tutorial status, selected character and sound settings in its private app storage. These records let you resume your climb and keep your settings.

SalNova Labs does not upload these gameplay save records to a developer server. Blastbelly has no player registration, account system, cloud-save service, chat, or separate developer-operated analytics or crash-reporting service. The Android build disables automatic app backup.

Advertising and Google AdMob

Blastbelly includes the Google Mobile Ads SDK through the Poing Studios AdMob plugin to request and display advertisements between chapters. Google processes advertising data as a third-party service provider under its own policies. Testing builds can use Google test advertisements; the advertising SDK can still process device and usage information.

The SDK automatically collects and shares IP addresses (which can indicate approximate location), interactions such as app launches, taps and ad video views, performance and diagnostic information, and advertising/device identifiers such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID. Where applicable, identifiers related to accounts signed in on the device may also be processed by Google. These are used for advertising, measurement and analytics, and fraud prevention.

We do not provide Google with your name or email through the game. We do not operate additional ad-network mediation adapters. Google and its advertising service partners handle information as described in the Google Privacy Policy and advertising technology information linked below.

Permissions and sensitive information

The game does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, photos, or precise GPS location. Advertising uses network access and related SDK permissions, including advertising identifiers and basic device/network information. Approximate location may be inferred from an IP address without a GPS permission.

The game does not ask you to enter a name, email address, payment information, or other personal details to play.

If you contact support

If you email us, we receive your email address, your message, and any information or attachments you choose to send. We use them to respond to your request and investigate issues. Support email is handled through our email provider, Google/Gmail. Please send only information needed for your request.

Sharing, security and international processing

Information is processed by Google for advertising and by our email provider when you contact support. Google may process information on servers outside your country under its privacy terms. We may disclose support information when required by applicable law or to protect rights and safety.

Local save files use Android app-private storage. Google states that data collected by its Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using TLS. No method of storage or transmission can guarantee absolute security.

Retention and deletion

Gameplay records remain in app storage until cleared or removed. To erase them, use Android Settings, open Blastbelly, and choose the option to clear app storage/data, or uninstall the app. This removes your local progress and settings. Starting a new climb resets the active chapter but is not a complete deletion of all saved unlocks.

Support messages are kept for as long as needed to answer and resolve requests and meet applicable legal obligations. To request deletion of support correspondence held by SalNova Labs, email the privacy contact above. We may retain information required by law.

Google controls retention of information processed by its advertising services under its own policies. Clearing or uninstalling Blastbelly does not itself delete information already held by Google. Use Google privacy controls or contact Google for requests concerning that information.

Your choices

You can use the advertising/privacy controls in Android settings, including resetting or deleting the advertising ID where available. The exact controls depend on your Android version and device. Core gameplay can be used without an internet connection; ads and the public policy website require connectivity.

For privacy questions or requests concerning information held by SalNova Labs, contact salnovalabs183547@gmail.com. Any rights to access, correct, delete or restrict use of information depend on applicable law and the information concerned.

Children and family privacy

Blastbelly does not provide player accounts or ask players to submit their age or contact details. The game has no separate child-profile mode. The advertising SDK processing described above may occur when ads are requested. Parents or guardians with questions about a child's privacy can contact us at the address above.
